Privacy Policy

How information should be handled when contacting the hospital.

Privacy information for this frontend demonstration

This demonstration website does not operate an appointment database. Its appointment and contact forms prepare an email in your email application; the information is not submitted to a website server by these forms.

Information you choose to share

If you send an email request, your name, contact information and any message content will be included in an email to the hospital. Avoid sharing highly sensitive medical details in ordinary email.

Third-party content

Google Maps, externally hosted illustrative photographs, fonts and icon scripts may involve their providers’ own data practices. A production deployment should review and minimize third-party services.

Before production launch

The hospital must approve a legally appropriate privacy policy, contact procedures, data retention rules, consent mechanisms, security measures, and patient data handling practices before enabling a live appointment backend.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries contact attariyahospital@gmail.com.
