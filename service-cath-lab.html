SPECIALIZED CARDIAC CARE

Sudurpashchim’s first reported Cath Lab service

On November 21, 2025, Attariya Hospital introduced a cardiac catheterization laboratory, reported by national and regional news organizations as the first of its kind in Sudurpashchim Province.

A Cath Lab is a specialized clinical facility used to diagnose and treat certain heart and blood-vessel conditions using catheter-based imaging and procedures. The most appropriate test or treatment depends on evaluation by a qualified medical team.

Cardiac procedures reported at launch

The services below were announced at the Cath Lab launch. Please confirm their current availability with the hospital before visiting.

Coronary & Peripheral Angiography

Catheter-based imaging to assess selected heart and blood vessels.

Angioplasty (PCI)

A catheter-based treatment option for certain narrowed or blocked coronary arteries.

Temporary Pacemaker

Short-term pacing support when clinically required.

Permanent Pacemaker

A cardiac rhythm device option for qualifying patients.

Plan your hospital visit

For specialist consultation, procedure suitability, preparation, schedules, and charges, contact the hospital directly. An online appointment request is not an emergency service or confirmed booking.
