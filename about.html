OUR STORY

Dedicated to caring for our community.

Attariya Hospital Pvt. Ltd. is located in Godawari Municipality–2 on Mahendranagar Road, Attariya, Kailali. Its existing website describes a mission to provide accessible, quality healthcare to its community.

The hospital’s public-facing website lists doctors across general surgery, medicine, paediatrics, orthopaedics, cardiology, radiology and other specialties.
